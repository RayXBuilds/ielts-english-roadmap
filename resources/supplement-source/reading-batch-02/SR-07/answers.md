## 语境词汇

- **quarry** 曾开采石料的采石场
- **extraction** 从现场开采取出材料
- **legible** 历史活动痕迹可被理解的
- **removable** 能拆走重新布置的
- **intervention** 对现场实施的改变
- **unanimous** 所有人一致同意的

## 长难句

> A combined project might eventually be possible, but it would still need to account for the time, space and resources required by its different purposes.

might保留可能性，but列出组合仍需满足的条件。

> Readers of the meeting record can therefore distinguish both the advisers' current positions and the circumstances under which those positions might reasonably develop.
