# SUP-S03 独立参考

## 原创回应

### 示例1

<!-- MODEL_START -->
Some consumers want information about origin because it helps them connect a product with qualities they care about. They may be interested in the distance it has travelled or in supporting a particular farming community. However, the name of a region alone cannot answer every question about quality. Useful information should be specific enough to help a person compare products without encouraging assumptions that all food from one place is identical.

Buying directly from a producer may allow a customer to ask detailed questions and hear how the food was grown. A large shop may offer a wider choice in one visit and more consistent opening hours. Which arrangement is preferable depends on the customer's priorities and access. Someone with little time might value convenience, while another person may enjoy speaking to the producer. Neither choice automatically shows that the customer cares more about food.

Labels could include more information when it helps people make a meaningful choice. For instance, a clear explanation of a production method may be more helpful than an attractive but vague phrase. The challenge is making the information readable. A short label could present the essential facts, with further details available elsewhere for those who want them.

Some people may ignore the information because price or availability has greater importance in their situation. Others may find technical descriptions difficult to interpret. That suggests two different responses. Clearer wording can help with understanding, but it cannot by itself solve the problem of a product being unaffordable.

## 错误改写与理由

教学原句

Everyone buys local food because local food is always better.

可用改写

Some buyers prefer local food because they want to support nearby producers. Quality still depends on the product and how it is handled.

撤回everyone/always，给可理解动机并限定质量推断。

教学原句

Shops have many products. Producers are people. It is different.

可用改写

Large shops can offer several product types in one visit, whereas buying directly may give customers more opportunity to ask about a particular product.

用同一比较维度的便利与信息机会连接，避免无关定义并列。
