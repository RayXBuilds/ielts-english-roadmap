# SUP-S02 独立参考

## 原创回应

### 示例1

<!-- MODEL_START -->
A sound from nature that I enjoy is the noise of rain falling on leaves. I remember hearing it clearly while waiting under the roof of a small shelter in a botanical garden. I had gone there for a walk, and a sudden shower made me stop for a few minutes.

At first, I mainly noticed the heavy drops hitting the roof above me. Then, as the rain became lighter, I could hear a softer sound coming from the trees beside the path. Different leaves seemed to produce slightly different noises, and occasionally a larger drop fell from one branch onto another. That small variation held my attention much more than a completely steady sound would have done.

I was standing still with my bag beside me, looking at the water gathering on the edge of a broad leaf. I had expected the delay to be annoying, but I found myself listening instead of checking the time. When the shower ended, I stayed for another moment before continuing my walk.

I enjoy this sound because it encourages me to notice details around me. I would not claim that rain always feels relaxing, especially if I were trying to get somewhere urgently. In that sheltered place, though, it turned an ordinary pause into something I remember pleasantly.

## 错误改写与理由

教学原句

The sound was nice. The place was nice. Everything was nice and I enjoyed it.

可用改写

As the shower weakened, I noticed separate drops falling between the leaves. Listening to those small changes made the wait enjoyable.

选择可观察动作与变化，解释enjoy而非重复形容词。

教学原句

I heard rain in a garden. Gardens are important for society and governments should build them.

可用改写

I heard rain while sheltering in a garden. I stayed still for a moment and noticed how the sound changed as the shower became lighter.

由公共政策回到题卡的个人观察与意义。
