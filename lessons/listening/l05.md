# L05 与 V05 Where Are You?

来源：VOA Learning English 自制 Let’s Learn English Level 1 第 5 课。基础情境材料，非雅思真题。

[对话 MP3](../../assets/voa/lesson-05.mp3) · [课程 MP4](../../assets/voa/lesson-05.mp4)

## 先听再回答

先关掉文本听一遍；较长音频分成 20～40 秒片段。首次听不懂时记录关键词，再逐段核对。视频是完整课程，下方文本只对应 Conversation 部分。

1. Where is Marsha at the start?
2. What do they do in the living room?
3. Which room does Marsha say she is in at the end?

<details>
<summary>展开官方对话文本</summary>

Anna: Hello, everyone! Today my friend Marsha is at her friend's house. She says it is beautiful. I want to see this house! Here we are!
Anna: Marsha, I am in the kitchen! It is a beautiful kitchen!
Marsha: It is beautiful. We cook in the kitchen.
Anna: I eat in the kitchen.
Marsha:  We relax in the living room.
Anna:  I relax in the living room. Marsha, let’s go upstairs!
Marsha: Anna? Where are you?
Anna: Marsha, I am in the bathroom! I wash in the bathroom.
Marsha: I am in the bedroom. We sleep in the bedroom.
Anna: I sleep in the bedroom!

</details>

<details>
<summary>查看练习参考答案</summary>

1. At her friend's house.
2. They relax there.
3. The bedroom.

</details>

## 输出练习

选一个角色，用自己的信息重说两句。然后让同伴改变一个条件，例如地点、时间或人物，练习听清后回应；独自学习时，隔一分钟再回答，不直接读稿。
