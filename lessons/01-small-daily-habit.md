# 原创练习 A Small Daily Habit

本项目原创短文，供恢复阅读习惯使用。建议作为 A1～A2 过渡练习，未经过正式等级校准。没有配套录制音频；听力训练请使用材料页的 L01 或 V01。

## 先读短文

Mia works in a busy office. She wants to read English books, but she often feels tired after work. One Monday, she decides to start with a small plan. She reads one short article after dinner. She does not look up every new word. First, she tries to understand the main idea.

Then Mia chooses three useful words and writes her own sentences. The next day, she reads the article again. This time, some sentences feel easier. On Friday, she tells her friend about the article in simple English. She makes a few mistakes, but her friend understands her.

Mia still has a lot to learn. She cannot understand every book or every video. However, she now knows what to do each evening. Her plan is small enough for a busy day, and she is ready to continue.

## 理解问题

1. When does Mia read?
2. Does she look up every new word?
3. What does she do with three useful words?
4. Who listens to her on Friday?
5. Why can she continue the plan on a busy day?

## 用自己的话表达

回答：What can you do for ten minutes after dinner? 先说三句，再写下来。可用结构：I want to … / I can … / Tomorrow, I will …

可复用表达：look up a word、the main idea、be ready to。各造一句与你自己的生活有关的句子，次日不看笔记重说。
