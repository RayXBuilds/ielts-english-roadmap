## SUP-S04 完整三部分即兴练习

原创专项，预计60分钟。所有示例为教学虚构文字。实际原音、用时和真人反馈均待学习者完成。

### 题目与首答

请同伴依次读题，或独练逐题揭示，首次连续录音11～14分钟。

Part 1，4～5分钟。依次提问，视时间用Why?补理由。
1. Do you enjoy looking at the sky?
2. When do you notice the weather most?
3. What do you usually do on a very windy day?
4. Do you prefer sunrise or sunset?
5. Do you ever look for stars at night?
6. Is the sky easy to see from where you live?

Part 2，3～4分钟含准备。
Describe a time you saw something unusual in the sky.
Say what you saw, where you were, who was with you, and explain how you reacted.
准备1分钟，长答1～2分钟。收尾从Was it easy to describe? / Would you like to see it again?选1题答20～30秒。

Part 3，4～5分钟。
1. Why are people interested in unusual natural events?
2. How can schools help children understand things they observe outdoors?
3. What difficulties arise when people explain an unfamiliar event online?
4. How does observing an event directly differ from watching a video?
选3个主问，每题60～70秒，再从后续分支选1题答30～40秒，读题与转题计入总时限。其余追问留重答。

### 练习步骤

准备2、首答11～14、完整回听14～17，合30；诊断10、参考5、重答10、归档5，另30，共60分钟。

保留全流程原音。回听先核题意和时间，再选一处语言证据。重答一个Part 1主问、完整Part 2及一个Part 3主问，用另一个追问分支检验迁移。

优先使用真实经历或真实观察。缺少相关经历可注明假设技能练习。时长按实际录音登记，示例词数只作文字计量。

## SUP-S04 独立参考

### 原创回应

#### 示例1


I prefer sunset because I am usually more relaxed at that time of day. If I have finished my work, I can stop and watch the colours change without feeling that I need to hurry. I rarely see sunrise, so my preference is partly a matter of routine.


实际口答文字词数48，录音时长待实测。

#### 示例2


I once saw a pale ring around the moon while walking home with my brother. We had left a relative's house after dinner, and the street was quiet enough for us to stop without getting in anyone's way. My brother looked up first and asked me whether I could see the circle too.

The ring was much larger than the moon itself. At first I wondered whether my glasses were causing the effect, so I took them off briefly and looked again. I could still see it. We stood beside a gate for a few minutes, trying to describe its shape and colour to each other. I took a photograph, although the ring was much less clear in the picture than it was to our eyes.

I felt curious because it was familiar and unfamiliar at the same time. I had seen the moon many times, but had never noticed that pattern around it. I did not know the explanation then, so I avoided telling other people that I was certain what caused it. Later, I looked for a reliable explanation of the phenomenon. What I remember most is the moment when an ordinary walk made both of us stop and pay attention.

#### 示例3


People may be interested in unusual natural events because they interrupt familiar expectations. Something they normally overlook suddenly seems worth examining. Schools can build on that curiosity by asking children to describe what they actually observed before suggesting explanations. This encourages attention to evidence. A video can help revisit an event, but its angle and image quality may hide details, so teachers should also discuss what the recording can and cannot show.


实际口答文字词数72，录音时长待实测。

### 分支回应示例

#### 分支1

It can, especially when an exciting explanation spreads quickly. People could compare reliable accounts and separate what the recording shows from what someone claims caused it.

#### 分支2

### 错误改写与理由

教学原句

I saw something unusual. It was unusual. We were very surprised. That is all.

可用改写

I saw a ring around the moon. My brother noticed it first, and we stopped beside a gate to compare what we could see.

加入观察者、动作、地点推进故事，最后再解释反应。

教学原句

A video proves everything because cameras never make mistakes.

可用改写

A video preserves a particular view, but the angle, lighting and editing can limit what we infer from it.

分清可见证据与解释，撤回绝对化。
