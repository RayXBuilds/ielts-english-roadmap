## SUP-W04 独立参考

以下为原创教学示例。自拟细节可替换，题干和图中事实须遵守。正式分数未确认，学生真实作品尚待提交。

### 完整范文

#### 范文1


Commercial advertisements are a familiar feature of streets and public squares. Although removing them could make these places less visually crowded, I do not support a complete ban. I favour clear limits on their size and location, together with stronger protection for places where people particularly need an unobstructed view.

The strongest argument for removal is that public space should remain usable and comfortable. Large advertising panels can compete with directional signs and dominate a small square. A person trying to find a bus stop, for example, may benefit more from a clear view of the route information than from another commercial message. Restricting advertisements around such information would serve a practical purpose. Similar limits could preserve the appearance of a historic street without assuming that every commercial sign is equally disruptive.

However, a general ban would also remove advertisements that have limited effects on the surrounding space. A small poster attached to an existing noticeboard might tell residents about a nearby service without blocking movement or covering a building. It would be difficult to justify treating this in exactly the same way as a large illuminated screen. Some advertising arrangements may also provide income for maintaining facilities, although cities should establish the actual value of that income rather than assume it always outweighs other concerns.

A reasonable objection is that rules allowing some advertising may be complicated to enforce. Clear categories could reduce that difficulty. Cities could specify protected locations, maximum dimensions and conditions for illuminated displays, then review complaints against those published rules. This would still require administration, but it would connect decisions to observable effects.

Overall, I agree that cities should reduce intrusive outdoor advertising. A selective approach would address the strongest problems while leaving room for modest signs that provide information with little disruption.

### 逐段修订与理由

#### 原稿1

I completely agree that all advertising must disappear. Small advertisements should be allowed everywhere because they are useful.

#### 修订1

I support removing intrusive advertising from sensitive public locations, while allowing modest signs where they cause little disruption.

①原稿all与allowed冲突。②限定范围形成一致立场。③替换后每段都需围绕intrusive/sensitive/limited effects展开。

#### 原稿2

Advertising is bad because it is terrible. Everyone hates it. One poster helped my friend, so no advertising should ever be restricted.

#### 修订2

Large panels can obscure directional signs, making a public square harder to navigate. A useful small poster shows that some advertisements have limited effects, but it does not remove the case for restricting obstructive displays.

①用遮挡→辨路困难代替同义循环。②撤回无证据everyone。③反例限定普遍主张的范围，不能从一个例子推出全部无限制。
