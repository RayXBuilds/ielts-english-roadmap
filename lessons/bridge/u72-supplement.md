## SUP-S01 Part 1用具体情境展开

原创专项，预计35分钟。所有示例为教学虚构文字。实际原音、用时和真人反馈均待学习者完成。

### 题目与首答

主题为衣物选择。
1. What kinds of clothes do you feel most comfortable wearing?
2. How do you decide what to wear on a busy day?
3. Do you prefer clothes with pockets?
4. Has your taste in colours changed?

每题答30～45秒。主问首答合计约3分钟，追问分支选一组，两问各30秒。

### 练习步骤

准备3、首答及追问5、回听7、参考和改写8、重答8、归档4，共35分钟。

直接回答→一个原因→一个生活情境。细节只需支持该题，追问第一句先回应新条件。示例人物偏好可替换。

优先使用真实经历或真实观察。缺少相关经历可注明假设技能练习。时长按实际录音登记，示例词数只作文字计量。

### 后续追问分支

#### 分支1　若主答强调方便

- Would you choose the same clothes for an important meeting?
- What would matter more than convenience then?

#### 分支2　若主答强调颜色或外观

- Can a colour affect how you feel?
- Could someone else react differently to the same colour?

由同伴按回答选择分支，独练时临时揭示。首次只用一支，重答用另一支。S04首答只取一问，重答再用另一支的一问，各30～40秒。

## SUP-S01 独立参考

### 原创回应

#### 示例1


I feel most comfortable in loose trousers and a light shirt, especially when I have to move between different places. For example, I would choose those clothes for a day involving both a train journey and a long walk. I might dress differently for a formal occasion, but comfort matters most in my ordinary routine.

On a busy day, I check the weather and think about where I need to go. If I will be outside for a while, I take an extra layer that fits in my bag. That saves me from having to return home just because the temperature drops.

Yes, I find pockets useful for small things that I need quickly, such as a travel card. However, I would not carry everything in them because full pockets can become uncomfortable. For a longer trip, I prefer keeping most of my belongings in a separate bag.

My taste has become a little simpler. I used to like combining several bright colours, whereas now I often choose one strong colour with something neutral. I still enjoy colourful clothes, particularly at informal celebrations, so the change is mainly about how I combine them.

### 错误改写与理由

教学原句

I like it because it is good. It is very good for me.

可用改写

I like jackets with inside pockets because I can reach my travel card quickly.

用具体衣物和用途替代循环good。

教学原句

Yesterday I buy a shirt and it fit me very well.

可用改写

Yesterday I bought a shirt, and it fitted me very well.

过去时间与动词一致，buy→bought，fit在此使用英式过去式fitted。
