## SUP-W03 G书信目的与礼貌请求

### 完整题面

You left a musical instrument on a coach after a journey. Write to the coach company's lost property officer. In your letter
- describe your journey and the item
- explain why you need the item back
- ask how it can be located and returned.

Begin Dear Sir or Madam,
Write at least 150 words. Spend about 20 minutes. You do not need to write any addresses.

角色、班次与物品细节可合理自拟。

## SUP-W03 独立参考

以下为原创教学示例。自拟细节可替换，题干和图中事实须遵守。正式分数未确认，学生真实作品尚待提交。

### 完整范文

#### 范文1


Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to ask for help locating a violin that I left on one of your coaches. I travelled from Hillford to Eastport on the service departing at nine on Monday morning. My seat was near the back, and I believe I left the instrument in the overhead luggage space above it.

The violin is inside a dark green case with a silver handle. There is a small white label bearing my surname on the side. I need it for a rehearsal next week, so I would be grateful if you could let me know whether it has been handed in. It is also an instrument I have used for several years and would be difficult for me to replace at short notice.

Could you check with the driver or the team responsible for cleaning that coach? If the case has been found, please explain what identification I should bring and where I can collect it. I can visit the depot on Friday afternoon, although I would appreciate knowing whether another collection time or a delivery arrangement is possible.

Yours faithfully,
Alex Chen


正文词数 180。书信剔除称呼及结束署名。

### 逐段修订与理由

#### 原稿1

Hey mate, you lost my violin. Send it now. I will arrive whenever I want and you must be there.

#### 修订1

I believe I left my violin on your coach. Could you let me know whether it has been found and what collection arrangements are available?

①对陌生工作人员用正式语气。②角色承认自己遗落，撤回无依据指责。③给可执行询问，避免未获确认就要求随时接待。

#### 原稿2

It is important. I need it. Please find my thing on your bus. Thank you very much for your help.

#### 修订2

The violin is in a dark green case with a silver handle. I travelled on Monday’s nine o’clock service from Hillford to Eastport and need the instrument for a rehearsal next week.

①补可识别物品与班次以支持寻找。②说明需要归还的原因。③这些为示例自拟信息，学生可用自己的合理角色设定。
